Android Puzzle Game

Oh My Blocks!

Oh My Blocks! is a crisp, strategic 8×8 block-placement puzzle game featuring Classic adaptive runs, deterministic UTC Daily Challenges, smart lookahead hints, bounded undos, visual themes, and local Top 10 records.
