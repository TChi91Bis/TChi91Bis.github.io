Oh My Blocks! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Oh My Blocks! is an 8×8 block-placement puzzle game. We respect your privacy and keep your game data on your device.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Oh My Blocks! stores the following data locally on your device solely to preserve your game progress and preferences:

  • Active board state, piece tray, score, undo history, and game mode (Classic / Daily Challenge).
  • Lifetime gameplay statistics, local Top 10 personal records, Daily Challenge streaks, and unlocked achievements.
  • User preferences (sound effects, haptic feedback, visual theme, difficulty profile, reduced motion, and tutorial completion status).

Oh My Blocks! does not require account creation and does not transmit your game saves or statistics to a developer-owned backend server.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

Oh My Blocks! uses Google AdMob (google_mobile_ads) to display advertisements (banners on non-gameplay screens, interstitials between completed games, and optional rewarded ads).

Depending on your region (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, and regulated US states), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may present a consent form on startup so you can choose whether to permit personalized advertising. You can update your consent preferences at any time from Settings → Privacy Settings when applicable in your region.

When ads are requested, Google AdMob and its mediation partners may collect and process device and usage signals (such as Advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, app interactions, and crash/diagnostic metrics) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Children's Privacy

Oh My Blocks! is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under the applicable age of digital consent without parental authorization.

4. Resetting Your Data

You can clear your saved lifetime statistics at any time from Settings → Reset Statistics, or clear all local app data by uninstalling the application.
