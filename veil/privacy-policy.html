100% On-Device Photo Processing

Veil Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.veilprivacy.veil_app · Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Veil is an Android photo privacy redaction application built around a simple rule: your photos never leave your device. This Privacy Policy explains what stays strictly on your phone, what temporary files Veil manages locally, and how optional Google Play Billing and Free-tier Google AdMob services operate.

1. Photos, Redaction, & On-Device AI

  • No photo uploads: All image editing, GPU redaction shaders (Blur, Pixelate, Solid, Triangle Mosaic, Frosted Glass), face detection, text recognition (OCR), and EXIF/metadata stripping run 100% locally on your Android device and work in Airplane Mode.
  • On-device ML Kit models: Smart Face Detection and Smart Sensitive Text Detection use bundled on-device Google ML Kit models. Neither images nor extracted OCR text are transmitted to Veil or third-party servers.
  • EXIF & container metadata removal: Exported images are re-encoded cleanly and audited locally to verify that EXIF, GPS coordinates, camera make/model, XMP, IPTC, JPEG comments, thumbnails, and PNG text/EXIF chunks have been stripped before saving or sharing.

2. Zero Broad Storage Permissions & Temporary Local Cache

  • No gallery-wide permissions: Veil does not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. Photos are accessed strictly one at a time via the Android Photo Picker or Android Share Sheet, and exported via Android MediaStore / Storage Access Framework.
  • Immediate private cache cleanup: Working copies stored inside Veil's app-private cache directory are deleted immediately when you discard edits or cleanly close an exported session (with automatic 24-hour expiration for crash-abandoned sessions).
  • Cloud backup disabled: Android Auto Backup and device-to-device backup transfers are explicitly disabled (android:allowBackup="false") so temporary drafts or cached images are never backed up to cloud storage.

3. Advertising & Consent (Free Tier Only)

The free version of Veil displays limited, non-personalized advertisements powered by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

  • EEA/UK GDPR Consent (UMP): Before initializing ads, Veil uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather and respect your consent choices. You may review or withdraw consent at any time inside the app via Settings → Ad Privacy & Consent Options.
  • Data processed by AdMob: When ads are enabled on the Free tier, Google AdMob may collect IP address, approximate diagnostic metrics, advertising or app-set identifiers, and ad interaction data for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Zero ads in Veil Pro: Once Veil Pro is unlocked, the AdMob SDK is shut down and is never initialized on future launches.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Veil offers an optional one-time lifetime upgrade (Veil Pro, SKU veil_pro_lifetime) processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. Veil does not collect, process, or store your payment card details or personal identity. Google Play provides Veil only with purchase status and entitlement confirmation so your Pro unlock works offline and can be restored on reinstalls.

5. Accounts, Analytics, & Children's Privacy

  • Veil requires no account, login, or email address.
  • Veil does not run proprietary analytics servers, crash-upload photo hooks, or user profiling databases.
  • Veil is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes & Contact

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or support requests, please open an issue or contact the developer via the project repository: github.com/TChi91Bis/veil_app.
